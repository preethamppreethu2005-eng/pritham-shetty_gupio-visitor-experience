Key UX Decisions That Make Check-in & Check-out Frictionless

1. Zero Download Barrier

Scanning the QR opens a responsive mobile web interface immediately. No App Store or Google Play download hurdle that causes 60%+ visitor drop-off.

2. Single-Glance Digital Pass

Instead of a printed sticker or thermal receipt that gets lost, visitors get an Apple Wallet-inspired digital pass with dynamic QR code and clear host details.

3. Autonomous 1-Tap Check-out

Traditional visitors leave without notifying reception. With SwiftPass, the active status screen has a persistent "Check Out" button that revokes the badge and logs exit time instantly.
